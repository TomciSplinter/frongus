    How it works

    1. Name it

      Type a name. Words like King, Laser, Baby or Magyar pick matching gear. Any other name gets its own random look.

    2. Make it yours

      Shuffle for a new look or pick every trait yourself: 31 hats, 21 eye styles, 33 skins and 61 flags.

    3. Launch it

      Connect a Solana wallet and approve the transaction. Your coin goes live on pump.fun, paired with $FRONGUS.
